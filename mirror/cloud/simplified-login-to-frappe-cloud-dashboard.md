# Login from Frappe apps

> This feature is only available for sites created through SaaS flow (i.e. [frappecloud.com/erpnext/signup](http://frappecloud.com/erpnext/signup)).

1.  Go to desk of your site.
2.  Click on `Login to Frappe Cloud` button inside the menu.

![Screenshot 2024-10-28 at 5.50.14 PM](https://docs.frappe.io/files/Screenshot%202024-10-28%20at%205.50.14%E2%80%AFPM.png "Screenshot 2024-10-28 at 5.50.14 PM.png")  
3\. It will take few seconds to initiate the login request, after that you will be presented a modal like this

![Screenshot 2024-10-28 at 5.51.08 PM](https://docs.frappe.io/files/Screenshot%202024-10-28%20at%205.51.08%E2%80%AFPM.png "Screenshot 2024-10-28 at 5.51.08 PM.png")  
4\. Check your mailbox. You will recieve an e-mail with a verification code.

![Screenshot 2024-10-28 at 5.51.51 PMb](https://docs.frappe.io/files/Screenshot%202024-10-28%20at%205.51.51%E2%80%AFPMdb8d68.png "Screenshot 2024-10-28 at 5.51.51 PMb.png")  
5\. Put the verification code in input box and click on `Verify`  
![Screenshot 2024-10-28 at 5.52.15 PM](https://docs.frappe.io/files/Screenshot%202024-10-28%20at%205.52.15%E2%80%AFPM.png "Screenshot 2024-10-28 at 5.52.15 PM.png")  
6\. You should be now logged-in and redirected to Frappe Cloud dashboard

![Screenshot 2024-10-28 at 5.52.58 PM](https://docs.frappe.io/files/Screenshot%202024-10-28%20at%205.52.58%E2%80%AFPM.png "Screenshot 2024-10-28 at 5.52.58 PM.png")  
7\. You will also get a `Login Successful` banner

![Screenshot 2024-10-28 at 5.52.51 PM](https://docs.frappe.io/files/Screenshot%202024-10-28%20at%205.52.51%E2%80%AFPM.png "Screenshot 2024-10-28 at 5.52.51 PM.png")

  

  

> Sometimes many browser block url redirection. In that case, there are two solutions -
> 
> 1.  Tap on `Click Here to login` link inside the modal.
> 
> ![Screenshot 2024-10-28 at 5.52.51 PM](https://docs.frappe.io/files/Screenshot%202024-10-28%20at%205.52.51%E2%80%AFPM77e6e1.png "Screenshot 2024-10-28 at 5.52.51 PM.png")  
> 2\. Your browser will show a message that popup has been blocked. This message can be vary based on the browser.
> 
> ![Screenshot 2024-10-28 at 5.52.36 PM](https://docs.frappe.io/files/Screenshot%202024-10-28%20at%205.52.36%E2%80%AFPM.png "Screenshot 2024-10-28 at 5.52.36 PM.png")
> 
> In case of firefox, you can choose `Show https://....` option.
