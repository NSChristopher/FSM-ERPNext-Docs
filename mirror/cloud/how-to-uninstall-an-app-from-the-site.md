# Uninstall an app from the site

Go to the Apps tab from your Frappe Cloud dashboard.![](https://docs.frappe.io/files/2ZmfimM.png)

  

Click on the three dots of the app you want to uninstall from your site.![](https://docs.frappe.io/files/2CxpKvg.png)

  

From the drop-down select uninstall.![](https://docs.frappe.io/files/moQ6fvZ.png)
