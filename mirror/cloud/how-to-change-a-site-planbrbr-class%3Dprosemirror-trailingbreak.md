# How to change a site plan<br><br class="ProseMirror-trailingBreak">

##### **To change a site plan, click on the change button from your Frappe Cloud site's dashboard on the overview tab.**

  

  

##### **Select the appropriate plan you want to change your site to and click on the change plan button to change your site plan to the newly selected one.**
