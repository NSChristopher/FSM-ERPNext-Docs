# Switching between Teams

You can switch easily between teams from the Team section in Settings.

![](https://docs.frappe.io/files/cTjeBOU.png)

If you want to leave a team, first switch to some other team and then click on the Switch button under the Change Team action.![](https://docs.frappe.io/files/fCpXfpP.png)

Rememeber you can't leave your own team(created when you signup) since your the owner of that team.
