# Apache Superset

> **Apache Superset** is an open-source modern data exploration and visualization platform.

1.  You need to first generate a database user for your site. Follow this [documentation](https://frappecloud.com/docs/database-users-and-permission-manager) to create a read-only user.

Typically, it takes a few seconds to configure the database user. Once it's complete, click on **View Credential**

![Screenshot 2025-01-31 at 1.15.44 PM](https://docs.frappe.io/files/Screenshot%202025-01-31%20at%201.15.44%20PM.png "Screenshot 2025-01-31 at 1.15.44 PM.png")  
2\. Open your Superset dashboard and go ahead with connecting a new database.

![Screenshot 2025-01-31 at 2.53.56 PM](https://docs.frappe.io/files/Screenshot%202025-01-31%20at%202.53.56%20PM.png "Screenshot 2025-01-31 at 2.53.56 PM.png")  
3\. Choose **MySQL** as database type.

![Screenshot 2025-01-31 at 2.54.49 PM](https://docs.frappe.io/files/Screenshot%202025-01-31%20at%202.54.49%20PM.png "Screenshot 2025-01-31 at 2.54.49 PM.png")  
4\. Fill in all the required details and ensure the **SSL** checkbox is checked. Then, proceed.

![Screenshot 2025-01-31 at 2.56.20 PM](https://docs.frappe.io/files/Screenshot%202025-01-31%20at%202.56.20%20PM.png "Screenshot 2025-01-31 at 2.56.20 PM.png")  
5\. You will see this message upon a successful connection.

![Screenshot 2025-01-31 at 3.19.46 PM](https://docs.frappe.io/files/Screenshot%202025-01-31%20at%203.19.46%20PM.png "Screenshot 2025-01-31 at 3.19.46 PM.png")  
6\. Now, you can refer to the [official documentation](https://superset.apache.org/) to create a dashboard in Superset.
