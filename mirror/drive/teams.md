### Create and Manage

You can create and edit teams by going to _Settings > Teams_ along with managing or inviting members.

There are three types of users:

1.  normal users,
2.  _managers_. By default, only the creator of a team is a manager. You can add other managers,
3.  _guests_, who only have read access
