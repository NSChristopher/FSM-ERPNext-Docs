1.  Click on the share button (middle one in the bottom navbar)
    
    ![Screenshot 2024-09-26 at 10 22 41 AM](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d7505f08-96ce-4b58-a920-eea223e568b6)

    ![Screenshot 2024-09-26 at 10 22 42 AM](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/acf4d19e-63e3-444c-9f9d-1289463300e9)

    ![Screenshot 2024-09-26 at 10 22 41 AM](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3f95257a-6fce-4b98-b90b-0f89233b5c8b)
